Kitty Swarm Privacy Policy

Dumpling Cat · support@dumplingcat.com

EnglishTiếng Việt

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Kitty Swarm is a puzzle game published by Dumpling Cat ("we", "us"), an independent developer based in Vietnam. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, who else receives it, and what you can do about it. We have tried to keep it short and plain.

If you have a question, write to support@dumplingcat.com.

1. The short version

  • You do not create an account. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your game progress is saved on your device. We do not run our own game servers.
  • The game uses two services from Google to understand how levels are played and to fix crashes: Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They receive device identifiers and usage information described below. No other company receives data from the game.
  • The current version shows no ads, sells nothing and uses no install attribution service. If these are added later, section 6 explains what will change, and we will update this policy before that version is released.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time by email (section 10).

2. Who is responsible

Dumpling Cat is responsible ("controller") for the information described here.

Dumpling Cat (independent developer), Vietnam
Email: support@dumplingcat.com

3. Information stored on your device

The game keeps a save file (progress.v1.json) and a few settings in the app's private storage on your phone. It contains:

  • your progress: current and highest level, cleared pictures (album), stars, coins, boosters, lives, timers, tutorials already seen;
  • a profile made automatically on first launch: a random Player ID (letters and digits), a default nickname such as "Kitty1234", the date you first played and the cat avatar you picked;
  • simple statistics (wins in a row, first-try wins);
  • your settings: sound, vibration, language, battery saver.

This file stays on your device. We do not receive it. Deleting the game deletes it. If your phone backs up app data (for example Android backup or an iCloud backup), the file may be included in that backup, which Google or Apple handle under your own account with them.

4. Information collected by services in the game

When you play, the current version uses these services. They collect information automatically, through their software kits (SDKs) built into the game. The game needs an internet connection for this; it still works offline.

Google Firebase Analytics (Google LLC)

  • What: an app-instance ID created for your installation; your in-game Player ID (see below); your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA only if you allow tracking on iOS); device model, operating system and version, language, app version; approximate location (country or city) derived from your IP address; game events such as "level started", "level won", "level stuck", "level retried", "level quit", "life lost", tutorial steps, "booster used", pictures added to the album, cat breeds unlocked and when the store's review prompt was shown; and events Firebase records by itself, such as first open, session start and length, and app updates.
  • Why: to see how many people play, where players give up, which levels are too hard or too easy, and how many come back the next days, so we can improve the game. Firebase is not linked to any advertising service in this version; we do not use this data to show you ads.

Google Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC)

  • What: crash reports: the error and where in the code it happened, the state of the game at that moment (such as the last game events before the crash), device model, operating system, free memory/disk, and a random Crashlytics installation ID.
  • Why: to find and fix crashes.

Player ID. Firebase Analytics data is linked to your in-game Player ID, the random ID shown in Settings (it is not your name or any account). We use it so we can find and delete your data when you ask (section 10). Crash reports from Crashlytics are not linked to your Player ID.

We do not use these services to collect your name, email, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, precise (GPS) location or any content you type.

6. Planned: attribution, ads and purchases (not in the game yet)

We plan to add these in a later version. Until then, test versions have no shop and show no ads; buttons that will later offer an optional ad give their bonus for free. Before a version with any of these is released, we will update this policy and the store privacy labels.

Install attribution. We plan to use AppsFlyer (AppsFlyer Ltd.) to learn which advertising campaign brought an install, so we can stop ads that do not work. It would collect your device's advertising ID (on iOS only if you allow tracking), other device identifiers set by your operating system (such as Apple's IDFV), IP address, device information, install time and the store referrer (which ad or link, if any, led to the install), and a few game milestones (for example finishing the tutorial or reaching a level). AppsFlyer may report installs and milestones to the ad network that showed the ad (for example Meta, Google or TikTok) so it can measure our campaign. It is not in the game yet; we will list it, the ad networks that receive its reports and how long it keeps data in sections 4, 5 and 9 before it is enabled. Policy: AppsFlyer.

Ads. The game will show rewarded ads (you choose to watch one for a bonus), full-screen ads between some levels and a banner, through AppLovin MAX and ad networks connected to it, starting with Google AdMob. Others may be added (for example Pangle, Mintegral, Unity Ads/ironSource, Meta Audience Network); we will list each one here before it is enabled. Ad networks collect your advertising ID, IP address, device information, approximate location and how you interact with ads, to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalise them.

  • Your consent: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game will ask for your choice through Google's consent form (UMP) before personalised ads load. You can change it later in Settings. On iOS, the game will ask your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you say no, your IDFA is not used and ads are not personalised with data from other companies' apps.
  • Remove Ads: a purchase will turn off full-screen and banner ads. Rewarded ads stay optional.
  • Policies: AppLovin, Google ads.

In-app purchases. Payments are handled by Google Play or Apple; we never see your card details. We receive a purchase receipt (product, price, time, order ID) to give you the item and restore it on a new install. We may use RevenueCat (privacy policy) or Unity IAP for this; this section will name the one we choose. Firebase Analytics also records purchase events (product and price).

Cloud save and notifications may come later. If they do, we will describe them here before release.

7. Legal bases (EEA, UK and similar laws)

  • Analytics and crash reports: our legitimate interest in running, fixing and improving the game, or your consent where the law requires it for reading or storing identifiers on your device.
  • Install attribution (later): our legitimate interest in knowing which advertising works, or your consent where the law requires it.
  • Personalised ads (later): your consent.
  • Purchases (later): performing our contract with you.
  • Answering your emails: legitimate interest, or performing what you asked.

You can withdraw consent at any time (section 10); this does not affect what happened before.

8. Children

Kitty Swarm is a puzzle game for teenagers and adults. It is not designed for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the higher age set by law in your country, such as 16 in some EU countries). The game uses analytics and, in later versions, advertising measurement and ads, which are not suitable for young children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has played the game and their data was collected, write to support@dumplingcat.com and we will delete it (section 10).

9. How long we keep information

  • Save file on your device: until you delete the game or its data.
  • Firebase Analytics: user-level event data is kept for 14 months, then deleted automatically; reports that show only totals (for example "1,000 players reached level 10") may be kept longer.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports and their identifiers are kept for 90 days.
  • Emails you send us: as long as needed to answer you, and no more than 2 years.
  • Services added later (section 6): listed here with how long they keep data before they are enabled.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Stop collection: you can reset or remove your advertising ID (Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads; iOS: tracking is off unless you allow it). This version has no in-game switch to turn analytics off; uninstalling the game stops all collection.
  • Access, correction, deletion, objection, portability: depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK GDPR, California's CCPA/CPRA, Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Law, the Philippines' Data Privacy Act), you may ask to see, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw consent.
  • How to ask: email support@dumplingcat.com with the subject "Privacy request" and the Player ID shown at the bottom of the Settings screen. We do not need any other proof of identity because we hold no name or email for you. We will answer within 30 days and ask Google to delete the Firebase Analytics data linked to that Player ID. Crash reports are not linked to it; they are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • California: we do not sell personal information, and this version does not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. If a later version measures ads with ad networks (section 6), that may count as "sharing" under California law; this section will then explain how to opt out. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.
  • Complaints: you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Security

Information sent by the game to these services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We limit access to the analytics and crash-report dashboards to ourselves and use two-step verification. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the game changes what it collects (for example when ads or purchases are added) and change the date at the top. For important changes we will also tell you in the game or the store notes.
