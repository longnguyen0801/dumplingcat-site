How to play

Every level is a pixel picture made of yarn blocks. Clear it before your five slots fill up.

  1. Tap a house

    It moves into a slot and its kittens run out to the picture.

  2. Fetch the yarn

    Kittens carry home every block of their colour they can reach from the edge.

  3. Finish the picture

    Plan the order, clear every block and add the picture to your album.
